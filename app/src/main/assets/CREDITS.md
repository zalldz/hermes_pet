Skin slime di `assets/art/slime_*.png` berasal dari **Kenney (www.kenney.nl)**,
paket **New Platformer Pack** (`Sprites/Enemies/Double/slime_{normal,fire,spike,block}_rest.png`),
lisensi **CC0 1.0** — http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Perubahan: dipotong, wajah bawaan dihapus (wajah digambar pet sendiri), dijadikan grayscale
(di-tint warna suasana hati saat runtime), lebar 112 px.
